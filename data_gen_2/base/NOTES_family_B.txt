## 4. Collision Rule Validations
- C1: All scenarios reference exclusively POs owned by Family B.
- C2b: Across each vendor, every open PO contains strictly disjoint SKU items. No item overlap within any vendor.
- C3: ZERO catalog items with `unique_item: true` are used in Family B. All 13 unique items remain completely available for Family R.
- C4: No invoices within 10 days have amounts within 0.5% of each other.
- C5: All invoice numbers per vendor have rapidfuzz ratio similarity < 0.85 (actual max 0.80).
- C8: All dates are offsets between -85 and 0, and all invoice dates are strictly >= PO dates.
- Output Hygiene: No derived fields (`subtotal`, `grand_total`, `tax_total`, `qty_remaining`, `norm`, `hash`) authored anywhere.
