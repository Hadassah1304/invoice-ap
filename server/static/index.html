arrow_right How is Match Confidence calculated?
Click to expand explanation

When an invoice is uploaded, our engine compares it against all open Purchase Orders (POs) from the same vendor to find the exact match. Each candidate PO is scored out of 100% using 5 weighted business checks:

35% Primary
Price Agreement
Do unit prices match the contracted rates in the PO?
30% Quantity
Quantity Headroom
Does the billed quantity fit within remaining unbilled units?
20% Budget
Budget Coverage
Does the invoice total stay within the remaining unbilled budget?
10% Catalog
Line Overlap
Do item descriptions and part names match PO items?
5% Timeline
Timeline & Dates
Was the invoice issued on or after the PO order date?
Confidence Thresholds: 80%–100%: High Fit (Ready for automatic straight-through approval) 50%–79%: Moderate Fit (Partial delivery or slight variation requiring review) < 50%: Low Fit (Overbilled, closed PO, or catalog mismatch)
