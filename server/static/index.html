How is Match Confidence calculated?

When an invoice arrives, candidate purchase orders from the same vendor are evaluated across 5 weighted business signals to calculate an overall Match Fit Percentage (0% to 100%):

Unit Price Alignment 35% Weight
Checks if invoice unit prices match the negotiated PO contract rates within tolerance.
Quantity Headroom 30% Weight
Ensures billed quantities fit within the unfulfilled, remaining items on the purchase order.
Budget Draw & Balance 20% Weight
Measures how invoice amount fits the PO commitment. Full billings score 100%; partial milestones score by the proportion drawn.
Line Item Overlap 10% Weight
Proportion of billed line items successfully recognized and matched against PO line specifications.
Chronology & Terms 5% Weight
Validates that invoice date post-dates the PO creation date and falls within vendor payment terms.
How to Interpret the Match Fit Score:
  • 80% – 100% (High Fit): Strong contract alignment. For partial deliveries with 100% price/qty alignment, scores in the 80s reflect a healthy partial draw against the open PO.
  • 50% – 79% (Moderate Fit): Requires reviewer attention; typically indicates minor price variances or partial line matching.
  • Below 50% (Low Fit / Rejected): Significant mismatches, unallocated catalog items, budget overrun, or closed purchase order.
